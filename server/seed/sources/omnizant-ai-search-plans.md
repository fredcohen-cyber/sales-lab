# Omnizant AI Search Plans

Omnizant’s AI search plans apply Symphony’s connected growth philosophy to a specific challenge: helping law firms get discovered and recommended, then connecting that attention to new client opportunities. This brief defines the story our teams should tell and the business outcomes that story must serve.

_Positioning Brief: Internal positioning for sales, marketing, and leadership. October 2026._

## Positioning in brief

**Offer:** Omnizant AI Search Plans

**Plans:** Advantage AI and Max AI

**Core message:** Get discovered and recommended by AI.

### The short version (internal)

Omnizant’s AI Search Plans strengthen how law firms are discovered, represented, and recommended through AI search.

The plans include Growth Studio to support lead capture, timely follow-up, and visibility into lead sources.

The goal is to help the firm attract qualified inquiries and turn more of those opportunities into retained clients. Being discovered and recommended by AI tools is a starting point in that process, and we assess its value in the context of client growth.

### The core positioning statement

**For law firms looking to bring in more clients, Omnizant’s AI Search Plans expand the ways prospective clients can discover and retain your firm through AI search, with Growth Studio included to capture more inquiries from website visitors and help you win more business. Together, these efforts build a connected path from discovery to retained.**

### The central belief

Being mentioned is only useful when it helps the right prospective clients move closer to choosing your firm. Our responsibility is to connect visibility to that larger goal.

### Who we are speaking to

Law firm owners and marketing decision-makers who want to understand how their firm appears in AI search and improve that presence. Some need a reliable foundation. Others want ongoing work to expand discovery. Both need a clear business reason to invest.

### The specific AI theme

**Get discovered and recommended.**

“Discovered” speaks to reaching prospective clients who may not already know the firm. “Recommended” speaks to becoming a credible option when someone asks for guidance about counsel. Together, they describe the role we want the firm to earn in AI-assisted discovery.

Treat recommendation as an objective we work toward. AI platforms control their own responses. Our language should describe strengthening the firm’s presence and supporting signals without promising inclusion or endorsement.

### Discovery can begin before a lawyer search

A prospective client may start with a question about an employment dispute, planning for a parent, or a change in family circumstances. The opportunity is to make the firm useful and credible during that research, as well as when someone explicitly looks for an attorney. These are illustrative journeys, not claims about measured search demand.

### How this connects to Symphony (and our other traditional SEO plans)

Symphony establishes the broader philosophy: connected marketing should help people find a firm, choose it, and become clients. These plans apply that philosophy to AI discovery. They should feel like part of the same approach to growth, with a defined scope focused on AI search visibility.

Use Symphony’s framework to explain the relationship. Do not imply that buying an AI plan includes every Symphony service or that the two offers are interchangeable.

## How we earn confidence

### The reasons to choose this approach

**A connected growth objective**. We begin with discovery and keep the path to retained clients in view. That gives the work a commercial purpose beyond improving a visibility report.

**A foundation that supports consideration.** The firm’s website content and information elsewhere should help people understand what the firm does and whether it fits their needs. The scope of that work depends on the plan.

**Growth Studio included.** Lead capture and follow-up are part of the offer’s story from the beginning. They give firms a practical way to act on interest when it arrives.

**Legal-specific judgment.** We frame visibility around the matters a firm handles and the people it serves. A relevant inquiry matters more than an appearance in an unrelated answer.

### What we measure and what it tells us

| Level | Evidence to examine | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Discoverability | Tracked prompts, appearances, accuracy, and relevant context. | Helps diagnose presence within the monitored sample. |
| Opportunity | Identifiable lead sources, suitable inquiries, and consultations where recorded. | Shows whether discovery is connected to opportunities the firm can act on. |
| Business outcome | Retained clients and associated revenue where reliably recorded. | Helps assess commercial contribution with the firm’s outcome data. |

AI visibility measurements are observations within a defined sample. Explain the prompts, platforms, dates, and method used. A mention does not establish that someone saw it, contacted the firm, or became a client.

Lead-source reporting can show identifiable referral activity. It cannot capture every influence on a person’s decision. Describe an AI referral as an observed source when supported; use “influenced” only when additional evidence supports it. Report revenue only when reliable matter or financial data is available.

### The proof we should develop

- Documented examples of inaccurate or incomplete firm information and the corrections made.
- Repeatable visibility observations with the measurement scope and limitations recorded.
- A Growth Studio walkthrough showing inquiry capture, source information, and follow-up.
- Permissioned client examples connecting visibility work to qualified opportunities, with outcomes verified where available.

## Messaging guidance for the team

### How to handle common questions

**“Is this another SEO plan?”**

Search fundamentals support the work. These plans focus on the firm’s AI search presence and include Growth Studio so visibility remains connected to lead capture and follow-up. Explain the scope of the selected plan rather than suggesting SEO has stopped mattering.

**“Will ChatGPT recommend my firm?”**

We work to strengthen the information and signals that support discovery and consideration. Each platform determines its own answers, so we cannot guarantee a recommendation.

**“How will I know whether it works?”**

We establish a baseline and examine changes within the tracked scope. We also connect the website to lead-source and conversion tracking. Where the firm records consultation and retention outcomes, we can assess those alongside visibility and inquiry data.

**“We already have a website and an agency.”**

Start by understanding what the firm wants to improve and where its current provider is falling short. Ask what they wish their agency did differently, whether they are getting enough qualified inquiries, and how clearly they can connect their investment to new clients.

Use those concerns to explain how Omnizant’s AI Search Plans could address the gaps. Ground the conversation in what the firm wants to achieve and why its current approach is leaving them dissatisfied.
