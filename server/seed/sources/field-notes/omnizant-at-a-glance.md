### The company

- Founded in 2006: about 20 years serving law firms.
- About 45 employees, with headquarters in Port Washington, New York, on Long Island. Reps on these calls were based in several states, though one described himself as one of the company’s few remote workers; confirm how to describe where the team works.
- Founded by Fred Cohen, an estate planning attorney, who is still president and CEO. Omnizant works only with attorneys; it is not a general marketing agency.
- A member benefit partner with the State Bar of Texas. One rep told a prospect the partnership requires Omnizant to provide value to members, so it occasionally does educational outreach such as the free website audit.
- Donna, the Director of Sales, has been with the company about 18 years.
- Many firms have switched to Omnizant from FindLaw since its sale, and from Scorpion and Justia.

### Websites and services

- Websites built on WordPress, ADA compliant and fast, which the firm owns from day one and can move to any host if it leaves.
- Website security, design, updates, and maintenance, with SEO available as a service.
- Management of the firm’s Google Business Profile, kept consistent with the website: images, colors, office hours, and practice areas.
- Lead capture through contact forms and a chat or pop-up, with instant alerts by email, text, and app notification, and one-click replies by call, text, or email.
- Tracking of where each lead came from, such as a search for the firm’s name, a directory listing, or a social media post, with the data owned by the firm.
- Support staff, and a library of attorney-written content.

### Switching from LawLytics

- More than 100 LawLytics attorneys have moved to Omnizant. LawLytics provides access to the site and its files, and the move takes 30 days or less.
