The free website review is how Omnizant’s outbound calls most often earn a first meeting. On fourteen recorded outbound calls, it was the meeting booked on twelve; the other two booked a call to go over Omnizant’s services.

### What it is

A 10- to 20-minute video call, usually Google Meet, sometimes with a shared screen, where the rep goes over what they found on the firm’s website and online presence and learns what the firm wants from its site. A website specialist or the Director of Sales sometimes joins. Reps promise it won’t be a high-pressure sales call: it is mainly informative, and the rep also tells the firm about Omnizant. Some reps add that if the firm wants Omnizant’s services, that will be a separate call.

### How reps describe it

- *“A review of your website: what’s working for you, and if anything needs improvement.”*
- *“Think of it like an MRI or a physical for your website.”*
- *“It’s something you could give to whoever is redoing your website, as a starting point.”*

### What it looks at

As reps describe it: page speed and load time; the site’s pages and whether they are properly linked; ADA accessibility; the Google Business Profile and reviews; and whether the firm’s name, details, and branding are consistent everywhere it appears online. Findings come with recommendations the firm can carry out itself, with another vendor, or with Omnizant.

### What the firm provides

Nothing but the meeting: no logins, access, or data. The review uses the public website and the firm’s public listings, and all a rep asks for is 15 minutes.

### Who it suits

- Firms whose website is with FindLaw, Justia, Scorpion, or LawLytics (see Competitor angles from Omnizant’s calls).
- The person who manages the website or the provider relationship, not only the named partner.
- Attorneys who don’t market online and use the site as a “digital business card”: the review becomes a credibility and health check, with SEO left out.
- Firms already planning a redesign or meeting other vendors: the findings are useful whoever does the work.
