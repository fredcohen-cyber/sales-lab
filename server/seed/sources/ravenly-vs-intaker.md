**Intaker** is an AI-powered legal intake and lead management platform tailored for law firms. It offers a suite of tools to streamline client interactions, qualify leads efficiently, and automate follow-ups, thereby improving conversion rates and client engagement.

### Where Intaker delivers value

Intaker publicly markets a broad intake platform: website chat, communications, workflows, and named AI agents. Respect it as a capable competitor, and never describe it as merely a chatbot.

- **Chat + video widgets.** Customizable website chat with video branding, visitor voice and video submissions, and live call connection.
- **Two-way texting.** Shared-number text threads with templates and file exchange, plus an AI texting agent that Intaker says qualifies, books, transfers, and follows up with inquiries.
- **Follow-up automation.** Prebuilt text and email sequences, and workflows triggered by chats, AI conversations, status, and qualification, with actions such as calls, messages, tasks, and pipeline changes.
- **Lead management dashboard.** A central place to view, track, and assign leads, with a shared inbox, tasks, pipelines, and routing.
- **CRM integrations.** Syncs captured information with the firm’s existing systems. Compare the specific events and fields the firm needs, not integration counts.
- **Analytics.** Intaker advertises reporting from lead source through to signed client.

### Why we win

These are Ravenly’s demonstrable strengths, not proven exclusive features. Show them in the buyer’s own scenarios rather than claiming Intaker cannot do the same.

#### 1. Intaker leads with conversations. Ravenly is conversion-first.

Intaker’s pitch centers on conversations: website chat, AI agents for texting and Google Local Services Ads, and two-way texting. Ravenly centers everything around **capturing the lead cleanly, qualifying it quickly, and helping the firm focus on who to follow up with first.**

Ravenly supports chat, but doesn’t force it. It offers **multiple conversion paths** that match how real prospects prefer to inquire, including short forms and structured data capture that feel more natural than a long chatbot conversation.

**Why it matters:** Prospects take different paths. Ravenly meets them where they are.

#### 2. Intaker’s plans follow website traffic. Ravenly’s follow leads.

In the pricing above, Intaker’s tiers are set by users and unique website views. Ravenly’s published plans are set mainly by monthly leads (phone calls and spam excluded), users, and practice areas; see **12 · Packaging, pricing, trial, and commercial discovery**. A traffic-based allowance grows with visitors whether or not they inquire, which can weigh on firms with strong SEO or ads. A lead-based allowance follows inquiries.

Confirm both vendors’ current terms, and compare lead definitions, implementation, usage, and contract length before calling either option cheaper.

**Why it matters:** Firms care about qualified opportunities, not traffic volume.

#### 3. Intaker advertises AI qualification. Ravenly shows the intake team why a lead deserves attention first.

Intaker promotes AI qualification in its texting and Local Services Ads agents, and its workflows can act on qualification status, so do not claim it has no lead scoring. Compare how each product helps the intake team decide who to call first.

Ravenly scores each inquiry using case-type priority and the firm’s own criteria, and shows the intake team an intelligence breakdown (intent, legitimacy, potential value, and case-type value) beside the original answers and an AI summary.

This helps your team move faster on the most promising opportunities.

**Important nuance:** Ravenly doesn’t replace intake. It simply ensures intake **starts with the right lead first**, with cleaner context. The firm’s criteria adjust the score; they do not automatically reject a lead.

#### 4. Ask how the team works in Intaker today. Ravenly keeps the daily workspace clean and immediately usable.

Intaker offers a broad feature set. Do not call its interface cluttered or hard to use; there is no verified source for that. Instead, ask which capabilities the team uses every week and where work still slows down, then let the intake lead work the same real inquiries in Ravenly.

Ravenly is intentionally designed to be **fast to scan and effortless to act on**, so staff aren’t clicking around trying to find what matters.

**Why it matters:** Intake teams already have complex workflows. Their tools shouldn’t add complexity.
