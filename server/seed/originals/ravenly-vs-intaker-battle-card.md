### Ravenly vs Intaker

**Intaker** is an AI-powered legal intake and lead management platform tailored for law firms. It offers a suite of tools to streamline client interactions, qualify leads efficiently, and automate follow-ups, thereby improving conversion rates and client engagement.

### Where Intaker Delivers Value

Intaker offers law firms a set of helpful tools for basic digital engagement. It is positioned well as a **chat-driven engagement tool with light automation** for firms that want to modernize communication.

* **Chat \+ video widgets**  
  Customizable chat interfaces (and optional video prompts) that try to pull visitors into a structured conversation.

* **Two-way texting**  
   A simple SMS channel to communicate with prospects and keep them engaged.

* **Follow-up automation**  
  Prebuilt text and email sequences designed to encourage leads to respond or take action.

* **Lead management dashboard**  
   A central place to view, track, and assign leads.

* **CRM integrations**  
   Syncs captured information with the firm’s existing systems.

* **Analytics**  
   Provides high-level visibility into channel performance and activity.

### Why We Win

## **1\. Intaker is chat-first. Ravenly is conversion-first.**

Intaker centers everything around a chatbot interface. Ravenly centers everything around **capturing the lead cleanly, qualifying it quickly, and helping the firm focus on who to follow up with first.**

Ravenly supports chat, but doesn’t force it. It offers **multiple conversion paths** that match how real prospects prefer to inquire, including short forms and structured data capture that feel more natural than a long chatbot conversation.

**Why it matters:**  
 Prospects take different paths. Ravenly meets them where they are.

## **2\. Intaker’s pricing is tied to website traffic. Ravenly ties value to meaningful outcomes.**

Traffic-based pricing penalizes firms with strong SEO or ads and forces firms to pay whether leads are qualified or not.

Ravenly’s model aligns with **lead quality and value**, not site visitor counts.

**Why it matters:**  
 Firms care about qualified opportunities, not traffic volume.

## **3\. No lead scoring in Intaker. Ravenly surfaces the best opportunities for the intake team.**

Intaker captures leads but treats them equally. Ravenly evaluates key data points to identify:

* urgency  
* fit  
* legitimacy   
* readiness to retain

This helps your team move faster on the most promising opportunities.

**Important nuance:**  
Ravenly doesn’t replace intake. It simply ensures intake **starts with the right lead first**, with cleaner context.

## **4\. User feedback points to Intaker’s busy UI. Ravenly keeps things clean and immediately usable.**

Intaker packs many features into its dashboard, but firms often find it cluttered or unintuitive.

Ravenly is intentionally designed to be **fast to scan and effortless to act on**, so staff aren’t clicking around trying to find what matters.

**Why it matters:**  
Intake teams already have complex workflows. Their tools shouldn’t add complexity.
